Payments

The Payments page lists every payment that's been recorded against your clients' sales invoices. It's also where you can record a new payment without having to open an invoice first — handy when one payment from a client needs to be split across several of their invoices.

Recording a payment

  1. Go to Billing › Payments.
  2. Click New Payment.
  3. Select the client. Their open invoices (anything sent or partially paid, with a balance still owing) appear below, each pre-filled with its full outstanding balance.
  4. Tick each invoice this payment should cover, and adjust the amount for any invoice it doesn't fully settle.
  5. Enter the date and, if you have one, a reference such as a bank transaction ID.
  6. Click Record Payment.

This records the same kind of payment as clicking Record Payment on a single invoice (see Sales Invoices) — the only difference is that here you can cover several invoices with one payment in one step.

Receipts

A PDF receipt is generated and emailed to the client automatically as soon as the payment is recorded. From the Payments list you can also View a payment's receipt at any time, or Send/Resend it if the client needs another copy.

Permissions

You need the can-record-payments permission to record a payment, and can-send-sales-invoices to send or resend a receipt. Ask your administrator if either action isn't available to you.
