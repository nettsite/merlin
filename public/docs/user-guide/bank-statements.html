Bank Statements

Bank Statements lets you upload your bank or credit card statement PDFs and have Merlin read the transactions automatically. Each transaction is listed as a line, with a suggested account code and — where Merlin spots one — a suggested matching invoice or payment.

Statements are for reconciliation, not posting Uploading a statement never changes your books by itself. It's a checklist: each transaction gets matched against something already recorded, or turned into a new entry on the spot. See Reconciling transactions.

Uploading a statement

  1. Go to Bank Statements in the navigation.
  2. Click Upload Statement.
  3. Select one or more PDF statement files.
  4. Choose the bank account this statement belongs to — this is the account in your chart of accounts that represents the bank or credit card (e.g. Business Current Account).
  5. Optionally select a bank template if one has been set up for your bank — this helps Merlin read your statement's layout more accurately.
  6. Click Upload. Merlin queues the files for processing.

Reviewing transactions

Once processed, click any statement in the list to open the detail panel. You will see:

  • Statement header information — bank name, account, period, opening and closing balances, and whether the extracted total reconciles against them.
  • A line per transaction — description, date, amount, and the account Merlin suggested.
  • Any transaction Merlin matched to an outstanding invoice or existing payment is shown with that suggestion, ready to confirm.

Reconciling transactions

Each line needs to be reconciled one of two ways before the statement can be closed out:

  • Confirm a match — where Merlin suggested a matching invoice or payment, click to confirm it (or use Confirm All Suggested to accept every suggestion on the statement at once). You can also match a line manually if Merlin didn't suggest one, or unmatch a line you confirmed by mistake.
  • Post to an account — for a transaction with nothing behind it, like a bank charge or interest, choose the GL account it belongs to and post it directly. This creates the missing entry and reconciles the line in one step.

If nothing already recorded matches a transaction — a payment you forgot to enter, say — create the missing document first (the same way you would anywhere else in Merlin), then come back and match the statement line against it.

Once every line is reconciled, mark the statement Reconciled to close it out.

Reprocessing

If the extraction wasn't right — for example, Merlin misclassified a category of transactions — you can reprocess the statement, as long as it isn't already marked Reconciled. Click Reprocess in the detail panel. A window appears where you can type an optional hint to guide Merlin (for example: Monthly debit order rows are insurance premiums, not bank charges). Merlin will re-read the statement and replace the existing transactions with a fresh extraction — any matches you'd already confirmed are cleared along with the old lines, so reprocess before you reconcile, not after.

Balance check

Merlin checks whether the sum of extracted transactions matches the difference between the opening and closing balance shown on the statement. If they don't match, the statement header flags it — this usually means Merlin missed some transactions or misread an amount. Review the lines and reprocess with a hint if needed.
