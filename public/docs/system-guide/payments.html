Payments

The Payments page (route payments.index, resources/views/livewire/pages/payments/index.blade.php) is a dedicated screen for recording a client payment, separate from the per-invoice Record Payment button on a single sales invoice (see Sales Invoices). It lists every existing payment Document (document_type = 'payment', direction = 'inbound') with its client, date, and amount, and lets a user generate or resend the PDF receipt for any of them.

Overview

Unlike the invoice's own Record Payment action, this page is client-first rather than invoice-first: pick a client, see all of their open (sent or partially paid, balance > 0) sales invoices, and allocate one payment across any subset of them. This is the UI for BillingService::recordPaymentForInvoices() — the same method backs both this page and the single-invoice Record Payment button, which simply calls it with one allocation.

Recording a payment across several invoices

Clicking New Payment opens a modal: select the client, then tick each open invoice to include and adjust its pre-filled allocation amount (defaults to that invoice's full balance due) if the payment doesn't cover it in full. Date and an optional reference are entered once for the whole payment, not per invoice.

On submit, recordPaymentForInvoices() requires at least one selected invoice, all invoices to belong to the same client, and every allocation amount to be greater than zero; it rejects the whole payment if the chosen contra account fails DocumentService::assertValidContraAccount(). It creates one payment Document for the full total (contra_account_id from BillingSettings::default_contra_account_id), linked to each invoice via DocumentRelationship (relationship_type = 'payment_for'), then calls DocumentService::recordPayment() per invoice to update its amount_paid/balance_due and flip its status to paid or partially_paid as appropriate. The whole operation is wrapped in a transaction so an overpayment or other rejection never leaves an orphaned payment document.

Receipts

Outside that transaction, BillingService::sendReceipt() fires automatically (see Billing Settings for the receipt-type BillingEmailTemplate) — a receipt failure is logged but does not roll back the committed payment. From the Payments list, View regenerates and opens the PDF receipt inline for any row, and Send/Resend re-triggers sendReceipt() on demand.

Permissions

  • documents-view-any — to view the Payments list (checked in mount() via $this->authorize('viewAny', Document::class))
  • can-record-payments — to open the New Payment modal and submit it
  • can-send-sales-invoices — to send or resend a receipt email
