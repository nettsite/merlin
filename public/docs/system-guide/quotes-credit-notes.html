Quotes & Credit Notes

Quotes and credit notes are the two remaining billing document types. Both reuse the shared Document / DocumentLine models — there are no separate tables — differing only in document_type, number prefix, and status machine. Quotes never post to the ledger. An issued credit note does — it's the only way to reverse a sent sales invoice, since invoices can no longer be voided.

Quote lifecycle

Statuses: draft → sent → accepted → converted, with declined and expired as alternative terminals. All transitions go through DocumentService:

  • sendQuote() — emails the quote and moves it to sent.
  • acceptQuote() / declineQuote() / expireQuote() — record the client's response.
  • convertQuoteToInvoice() — creates a new sales_invoice document copying the party and lines, gives it its own SINV number, links the two documents, and marks the quote converted. Returns the new invoice.

Credit note lifecycle

Statuses: draft → issued → applied. There is no voided status — a credit note is itself the reversal instrument, so it can't be reversed the same way an invoice used to be; once issued it's permanent (see The Ledger & Manual Journals on document immutability).

  • issueCreditNote() — finalises the draft; amounts are fixed from this point, and the credit note now posts to the ledger (it doesn't need to be applied to an invoice first).
  • applyCreditNote(Document $creditNote, Document $invoice, ...) — applies the credit against a sales invoice, reducing its outstanding balance, and links the documents. Rejects a credit note dated before the invoice it credits — the reversal must land in the period it's discovered, never backdated into the invoice's own period.

Reversing a sales invoice

A sent sales invoice can't be edited or voided. To correct one, use Issue Credit Note on the invoice — DocumentService::createCreditNoteFromInvoice() creates a draft credit note pre-filled from the invoice (party, currency, a like-for-like copy of every line), ready to issue as-is for a full credit or trimmed first for a partial one. The credit-notes page opens straight to this draft (#[Url(as: 'open')] handoff) so the operator finishes it in one flow.

Header edits only in draft Both page components allow header and line editing only while the document is in draft. Every mutating Livewire action re-checks authorization independently, per the project's Livewire security rules (#[Locked] IDs, authorize() in each action).

Relationship to accounts and reports

Quotes never appear in financial reports or the ledger. An issued credit note posts its own reversing entry — debiting the income account(s) it credits, crediting accounts receivable — independently of the invoice it's applied to. Every report summing a signed total for an account must sum credit − debit, not one side alone, or the credit note's effect goes unseen. See The Ledger & Manual Journals.
